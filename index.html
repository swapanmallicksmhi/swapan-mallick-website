01 / About

Weather science, observations and AI.

I am a Senior Research Scientist at the Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute (SMHI), working on numerical weather prediction and data assimilation. My research combines atmospheric models, satellite observations, radiative transfer modelling and machine learning.

My work spans variational and ensemble data assimilation, observation processing, background-error modelling, microwave and infrared radiance assimilation, regional reanalysis, and generative AI methods for weather applications.
